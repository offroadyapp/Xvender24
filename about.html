About Xvender24

Technology for Autonomous Retail

Xvender24 is engaged in the research and development of proprietary software, automation and IoT technologies in British Columbia for autonomous multi-vendor retail environments.

The company is developing an integrated technology platform that enables vendor management, inventory monitoring, customer membership and loyalty management, automated transaction allocation and settlement, remote site management, data analytics, and connected-device integration.

The platform is designed to support both merchant-side operations and customer-facing digital engagement across multiple autonomous retail locations.
