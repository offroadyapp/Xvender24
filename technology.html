Xvender24 Technology

One integrated platform. Six layers of technology.

Xvender24 conducts the research and development of its proprietary technology in British Columbia, Canada.

Software, automation and IoT infrastructure for multi-vendor autonomous retail environments.

Platform Architecture

The Xvender24 platform is an integrated technology stack covering vendor management, inventory monitoring, customer membership and loyalty, automated transaction allocation and settlement, remote site management, data analytics, and connected-device integration — designed for multi-vendor autonomous retail environments.

Technology Layers

01

Vendor Layer

Onboarding, catalogue, product allocation and vendor-facing dashboards for managing products across multiple sites.

02

Customer Layer

Digital membership, identity, controlled access and loyalty engagement for customer-facing applications.

03

Transaction Layer

Payment processing, automated transaction allocation, settlement and reconciliation across vendors and sites.

04

Operations Layer

Remote site management, monitoring, control, diagnostics and alerting for deployed locations.

05

IoT Layer

Connected-device integration, telemetry and hardware interfaces across smart retail terminals.

06

Analytics Layer

Demand forecasting, inventory optimization, product performance analytics and customer behaviour analytics.
